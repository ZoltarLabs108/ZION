Methodology notes

The boards (US markets, precious metals, sectors). The first column is the CASSANDRA predicted price — the median of similarity-weighted historical analogues, with the p10–p90 band beneath. Where too few analogues clear the similarity floor it falls back to a statistical random-walk band (tagged “stat”); where the predicted per-month move is too wide to trust, the price is gated (shown for reference only) — but the validated directional call still stands. Conviction (★ of 5) is the cascade's convergence value (valb) at the current frozen row, mapped to bands calibrated on a walk-forward out-of-sample test: the 5★ band (valb ≥ 0.875, ~13–15% of picks) earned +9.96%/6mo at 77.8% hit / OOS Sortino 4.86 — the only tier that decisively tops realized results (a backtest-Sortino candidate inverted out-of-sample and was rejected). No cell firing → 0★ → ABSTAIN. Call is the standing recommendation — each frozen cell classified on the newest complete-data row and carried forward until new macro lands (the current month's inputs publish mid-cycle, so the live call is the last fully-published month's, dated on each row). LONG/BUY · SHORT/SELL carry the cell's valb; otherwise ABSTAIN. On sectors, Hedge is index-conditional: HOLD LONG shows only while the S&P is predicted LONG, else it flips to HEDGE (S&P currently LONG).

Decorrelated badge. A ◈ decorrelated tag marks low-beta assets (|β| ≤ 0.35 — e.g. Gold β≈0.03, Platinum β≈0.30). It is a portfolio-diversification virtue (a decorrelated edge diversifies the book), shown as its own badge and deliberately not folded into the conviction star — the out-of-sample test showed conviction (valb), not independence, is what ranks results.

Stock Picks (21-day). Names that cleared the board scanner's walk-forward gate on the 21-day horizon — Bayesian probability, board out-of-sample accuracy, and the CASSANDRA expected move; options-greeks driven, forward not backtest. Gated on Bayes ≥ 65% and board OOS ≥ 60%. The CASSANDRA sign is shown as context, not a gate (a backtest found it has no forward value on stocks), so picks where CASSANDRA disagrees are flagged ⚠ shadow (kept on the board, tracked on the forward tape) rather than dropped. A pick also confirmed by the ODYSSEY 5-day waveform (momentum-up) carries a 🔥 flame — a second when its CASSANDRA confidence is HIGH.
